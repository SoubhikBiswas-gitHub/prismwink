# Reviewer instructions

No Prismwink account or login is required. A Google account may be needed for Google Meet's own meeting flow; the separate practice studio does not require one.

1. Install the package, click the extension icon and open Practice studio. Demo is clearly labeled and starts without camera/microphone capture. Choose any of the four effects and use Vanish, then Return to camera.
2. Choose My camera and allow Chrome's camera request. Use Capture empty room, step out during the five-second countdown, then return to the frame. Try a deliberate thumb–middle-finger contact, briefly hold, then release. A button and V key provide alternatives.
3. The optional studio microphone test starts only on an explicit button click. It does not transmit or play audio. Vanish mutes the local track; an explicit keep-muted choice is preserved on return. Stop camera & microphone ends the studio capture.
4. Open Google Meet and reload the tab after installation. Turn on the camera and use the Prismwink dock to capture the empty room. A normal call should be checked from another participant's receiving view. Vanish immediately gates microphone tracks; the visible Meet control is synchronized separately. Returning restores only prior microphone state owned by the extension.
5. If the microphone was already muted or manually changed, verify that return does not unmute it. Safe recovery also keeps audio muted. Compact dock mode retains safe recovery access.
6. The room plate is a still capture held in memory. Camera processing stays active while hidden to detect the return gesture. Stop processing or disable the camera to end camera capture.

All executable processing resources are packaged in the ZIP. There is no remotely hosted executable code, extra backend, paywall or activation code.

Release evidence: local automated receiving-media tests are available in the repository. They are not evidence of a completed live Google Meet acceptance call; that check must be completed before submission.
