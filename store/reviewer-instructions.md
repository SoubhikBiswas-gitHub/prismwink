# Reviewer instructions

No Prismwink account is needed. Google Meet needs a Google account for its own meeting flow.

1. Install the package and open a Google Meet call (or the pre-join screen) with the camera and microphone on. Reload a Meet tab that was open before installing.
2. A small Prismwink pill appears in the lower-left corner. After a few seconds it reads "Snap to vanish".
3. Click **Vanish** in the pill, or press Alt+Shift+V. The camera image dissolves into dust, the room remains, and Meet's microphone button switches to muted.
4. Click **Return**, or press Alt+Shift+V again. The image reassembles and the microphone turns back on.
5. To try the gesture, raise a hand facing the camera, press thumb and middle finger together, and snap (or flick them apart quickly). The pill shows "Hand seen", then "Now snap".
6. Mute the microphone in Meet first, then vanish and return: the microphone stays muted.

All code, WebAssembly and models are inside the package. There is no remote code, backend, paywall or activation step.
